**Project Overview:**

The final project is the highlight of the quarter: your opportunity to apply everything you've learned in CS123 to create something unique with Pupper. You'll have five weeks to develop, implement, and present your project.

**Project Timeline:**

Week 7 (11/2-11/6):
    - **Meet with a TA** for 15 minutes, in person or over Zoom, between **11/2 and 11/6** (at least two group members must attend)
    - Project proposal due **Friday, 11/6 at 11:59 PM** on Gradescope
    - Check out some project ideas `here <https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1vTYDniGllDyuABr1BxE2CVKiqiauncjWslB0OwzPIbk/edit?usp=sharing>`_

Weeks 8-9 (11/9-11/20):
    - Work on your project
    - Progress checkpoint **Monday, 11/16**: demonstrate your current progress to the instructors or TAs
    - OHs available for project support

Thanksgiving Break (11/23-11/27):
    - Limited support available
    - Continue implementation

Week 10 & Finals Week (11/30-12/9):
    - Final demo video due **Wednesday, 12/2 at 11:59 PM** on Gradescope
    - **Demo Day: Thursday, 12/3 at 6:30 PM, CoDA B90**
    - Final project wrap-up (code submission, blog post, etc.) due **Wednesday, 12/9 at 11:59 PM**

**Brainstorm Session with a TA (11/2-11/6):**
    - Before the meeting, brainstorm and list up to three project ideas.
    - In the meeting, discuss project scope and feasibility, any help or mentorship you need, and any extra hardware you need (so it can be ordered early).

**Written Proposal (due 11/6):** One page, on a single project idea, with these sections:
    - **Problem Description:** the problem you aim to solve.
    - **Proposed Solution:** your approach, and what you need (hardware, API credits, software, etc.).
    - **Expected Results:** what you plan to demonstrate on Demo Day.
    - **Milestones:** a *target goal* (baseline functionality), a *reach goal* (extended features) and a *dream goal* (the ideal full version). Say which milestone you expect to reach by the progress checkpoint on 11/16.

**Final Demo Video (due 12/2):**
    - Under 3 minutes.
    - Explain each demo out loud as you show it; no slides or annotations are needed.
    - The video must clearly and convincingly show that your project works.

**Demo Day (12/3, 6:30 PM, CoDA B90):**
    - Present to the teaching team, alumni, researchers and industry guests.
    - Each team has 5 minutes. Include a live demo; a slide deck is recommended.
    - Have a GitHub repo that documents your project, with installation instructions (e.g. ``requirements.txt``) so others can run your code.

**Grading Breakdown:**
    - Project Proposal: 20%
    - Progress Checkpoint: 20%
    - Final Demo Video: 20%
    - Demo Day Presentation: 30%
    - Final Project Wrap-up (code, blog post, etc.): 10%

**Important Notes:**
    - **No late days** allowed for any final project components
    - Failure to submit on time results in **0** for that component
    - Regular office hours available for project support
    - Extra hardware requests must be discussed during TA meetings
    - Using AI coding tools is encouraged: ROS 2 isn't the easiest system to work with, and your project will probably need packages you haven't used before.
